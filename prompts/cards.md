- Change a card with `gh project item-edit`, as before: the change goes to GitHub at once. Give it the ids from `{api}/boards` (`--project-id`, `--id` from the card's `item_id`, `--field-id`, `--single-select-option-id`), so it looks nothing up. Then `curl -s "{api}/boards?fresh=1"` reads the boards into the copy again, at most once a minute: a change can take up to a minute to show there. `gh` exiting with 0 means the change is on GitHub, even while `/boards` still shows the old value.
